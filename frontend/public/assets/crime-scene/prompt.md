# Prompt do asset: policiais de 1890

Create a transparent-background pixel art game asset: TWO complete standing Brazilian city police officers from Rio de Janeiro around 1890, in period-appropriate dark navy long tunics, high collars, brass buttons, simple kepi caps, belts, dark trousers and boots. Full bodies head to boots visible, standing calmly at ease, one slightly turned toward the other, not aggressive, no weapons raised. Distinct silhouettes, cohesive 16-bit pixel art with crisp deliberate pixels, limited muted palette matching a warm hand-painted pixel art city game. Both officers in one horizontal composition with generous transparent space around them, clean transparent cutout, no ground shadow, no scenery, absolutely no words, letters, signage, watermark, UI, or border. Designed to be scaled down to about 90 pixels tall in a 2D side-scrolling scene.

The result is trimmed to the alpha content and presented with nearest-neighbor scaling in the game. The warning board and its text are drawn natively in Phaser so the exact Portuguese wording remains legible.
